Slotsloader adds extra costume slots to Street Fighter 6: a costume mod becomes a
new outfit on the character select screen instead of replacing one you have.

INSTALL
- Easiest: Slotsloader Manager, Settings > Game files & replacements > Install
  Slotsloader (https://github.com/ZZtaii/slotsloader-manager/releases).
- By hand or with another mod manager: close SF6 and disable any older loader
  entry first (only one loader at a time). Install this zip as a tool mod, or copy
  its contents into the game folder (the one with StreetFighter6.exe).
  REFramework must be installed. The two DLLs belong together.

ADD OUTFITS
Put costume mods, as downloaded (.zip, .7z, .rar or a folder), into
reframework\costume_mods\<Character>\ and start the game. The first launch
builds the outfit PAK (re_chunk_000.pak.patch_NNN.pak), which takes a moment.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Up to 100 extra outfits per fighter. Bundles with options become one outfit
  per combination (up to 50 per mod).
- Slots made from Outfit 2, 3 or 4 show only when you own that outfit; Outfit 1
  and DriveTech (C5) slots always show.
- After a game update the loader rebuilds its data from the game's own files.
- SF6_CostumeLoader.log lists what was loaded and why something was skipped.

UNINSTALL
Close SF6 and remove it with the tool you installed it with (Slotsloader Manager:
Uninstall). Your costume_mods archives stay where they are.
